Lifting = brakes for my brain. What the hour under the bar does to the rest of your day. Hook seed: "This workout isn't for my arms."

3 · Late-Diagnosis Log

Did you miss it? So did 8 million other adults. More than half of adults with ADHD were diagnosed as adults. Your version of finding out.

Verified: CDC MMWR (2024), 15.5M US adults with current ADHD; 55.9% diagnosed at 18+ (≈8.7M).

Guys get caught in school. Unless they don't. Men are less often diagnosed as adults than women. What being the exception is like.

Verified: ADDitude's coverage of the CDC data, 40% of men vs 61% of women diagnosed in adulthood.

What the diagnosis actually changed. One concrete thing that's different, one that isn't. No medical claims.

Verified: about 1 in 3 adults with current ADHD get no treatment (CDC MMWR 2024). Use it only as context, not advice.
